About Me

I am a lecturer and master’s supervisor in the School of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence at Wuhan Textile University. I also conduct postdoctoral research at the Intelligent Transportation Systems Research Center of Wuhan University of Technology. I received my Ph.D. in Transportation Information Engineering and Control from Wuhan University of Technology in October 2022 and joined Wuhan Textile University in November of the same year. My research focuses on perception and localization for autonomous driving and robotics, including multisensor fusion, visual SLAM, and machine learning. I lead research projects supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China and other funding programs. I also review for academic journals and mentor students in competitions.

Research Interests

Autonomous Driving; Collaborative Robot Perception; Visual SLAM; Deep / Machine Learning
